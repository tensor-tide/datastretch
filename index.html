For Android

Make your mobile data last the whole month.

Data Pace keeps an eye on your data budget and, when you are using it too fast, gently slows downloads until you are back on pace. Auto mode picks the speed from your budget, and you can set your own rules for each app. You reach the end of the month with data left, instead of a throttled phone in week three.

How it works

  1. Set your budget

    Enter your plan's data allowance and the day your cycle starts.

  2. See where it goes

    Data Pace reads Android's own network statistics and shows how much mobile data each app used, today and in past cycles.

  3. Stay on pace

    When you are ahead of pace, a local VPN on the phone slows or holds back downloads, following the speed limits and rules you set per app.

What you get

Per-app counts

Mobile data per app, today and over 13 months of history, kept on the phone.

Speed limits and rules

Set speed limits and rules for each app, so the ones you care about keep working.

Helpful notifications

A heads-up when speeds change or a new cycle starts. You choose which kinds you get.

Destinations per app

Optional: see which internet hosts each app talks to, and how much data went to each.

Share your month

Draw a picture of your month or week in data and share it, with or without the amounts.

Export to CSV

Save your counts as a spreadsheet file in your Downloads folder.

Questions

Why does Data Pace need a VPN?

Android lets an app slow other apps' traffic only through its VPN interface. Data Pace's VPN runs entirely on the phone: traffic passes through the app straight to your carrier or Wi-Fi, never through a server.

Can I still use another VPN?

Android allows one VPN app at a time. While another VPN app is on, Data Pace keeps counting, but it cannot slow anything.

Does it read my traffic?

No content. It reads only what pacing needs: which app opened a connection, the address, the amounts, and how long name lookups take. Host names are read only if you switch on Destinations per app.

What happens after a reboot?

If pacing was on, it resumes by itself after a reboot or an app update.

Does it count Wi-Fi?

Data Pace counts your mobile data, since that is what your plan limits. If your Wi-Fi is metered too, you can switch on Treat Wi-Fi as mobile data in Settings.
