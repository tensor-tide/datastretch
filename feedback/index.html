Thank you for trying Data Stretch. We read every message, and a short note is just as welcome as a long one.

Write us an email

The address is attunesoftware@proton.me.

What helps us most

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if something looks wrong.
  • Your phone's model and Android version.
  • The app's version, which you find in Data Stretch under Settings › Your data, about › About Data Stretch.

When something went wrong

Data Stretch can attach a short log of what it did: open Settings › Your data, about › Share a log with support. You see the whole log before it leaves your phone, and you choose how to send it. The shared log carries no app names, no amounts and no budget.

Was uns am meisten hilft

  • Was du getan hast, was du erwartet hast und was stattdessen passiert ist.
  • Ein Screenshot, wenn etwas falsch aussieht.

Wenn etwas schiefgegangen ist

Data Stretch kann ein kurzes Protokoll dessen beifügen, was die App getan hat: Öffne Einstellungen › Deine Daten, Info › Protokoll an den Support senden. Du siehst das ganze Protokoll, bevor es dein Handy verlässt, und du entscheidest, wie du es sendest. Das geteilte Protokoll enthält keine App-Namen, keine Mengen und kein Budget.
